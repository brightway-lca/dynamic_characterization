Dynamic characterization functions as originally created for bw_timex. These base on the IPCC's AR6 and cover the radiative forcing for the 247 GHGs considered there.

## `"ar6"` parameter set

`ar6_core.py`, `gases.py` and `make_characterization_function` in `radiative_forcing.py`
reproduce the IPCC AR6 WG1 emission metrics (Forster et al. 2021, Section 7.6 and 7.SM.5,
Tables 7.15 and 7.SM.7), following the AR6 metric code
(https://github.com/chrisroadmap/ar6, commit 24f279260fc2d1ebd09b2bd4114f2be11859e925,
`src/ar6/metrics` and notebook `335_chapter7_generate_metrics.ipynb`, MIT licence).

Data:

- `data/ar6_parameters.json`: generated by `dev/build_ar6_data.py`; each section has a
  `_source`. Radiative efficiencies of CO2, CH4 and N2O are the Meinshausen et al. (2020)
  forcing at the 2019 concentrations of the AR6 code, with the AR6 adjustments (CO2 +5 %, CH4
  −14 % plus ozone and stratospheric water vapour, N2O +7 % plus ozone and its CH4 effect).
  Temperature response: AR6 two-layer emulator (q, d of notebook 335). Climate–carbon feedback:
  Gasser et al. (2017), Table 1 (mean = AR6; upper/lower = ±1 SD).
- `data/hodnebrog20.csv`: halocarbon lifetimes, radiative efficiencies and molar masses
  (Hodnebrog et al. 2020); identical to the input file of the AR6 code. Two blank CAS numbers
  are taken from AR6 Table 7.SM.7 and one CAS typo is corrected (see `gases.py`, `flows.py`).
- `data/decay_multipliers.json`: precomputed forcing series of the `"legacy"` functions.

Differences from the AR6 code (Table 7.SM.7):

- SF6 uses the lifetime of 1000 yr of AR6 Table 7.15 (GWP100 24 300, as in the static IPCC 2021
  methods) instead of the 3200 yr of Table 7.SM.7 (GWP100 25 200); see `lifetime_overrides` in
  `ar6_parameters.json`.
- The CO2 from the oxidation of fossil CH4 (AR6 Eq. 7.SM.5.8; yield 0.75, OH lifetime 9.7 yr),
  which the Table 7.SM.7 code does not contain, is computed analytically and without the
  climate–carbon feedback (already part of the CO2 impulse response). This reproduces Table 7.15
  (fossil 82.5 / 29.8 / 10.0, non-fossil 79.7 / 27.0 / 7.2).

### Indirect (precursor) gases

`data/precursor_parameters.json` (generated by `dev/build_ar6_data.py`) holds published
parameter sets for CO, VOC, H2, NOx and nitrate: per forcing component the steady-state forcing
per unit sustained emission, its response time and their sources, the precursor lifetime, the
mass basis, a `status` (`literature`, `literature-mean`, `fallback`) and the published GWPs that
the tests reproduce.

- Fry et al. 2012 (doi:10.1029/2011JD017134), "4 regions", 11 HTAP models: CO, NMVOC (kg C), NOx
  (kg N). Forcing from Table S1 and Table 3, short/long O3 split from the Table S2 GWP ratios.
  Sulfate: the Table S1 short-term forcing; Table S2 additionally contains a long-term sulfate
  response (difference < 2 % of the CO and VOC totals; larger for NOx).
- Fuglestvedt et al. 2010 (doi:10.1016/j.atmosenv.2009.04.044), Table 3: CO from Derwent et al.
  2001 and Berntsen et al. 2005 (UiO, LMDz), VOC from Collins et al. 2002.
- Warwick et al. 2023 (doi:10.5194/acp-23-13451-2023), Eq. 5 and Supplement Table S1, and Sand et
  al. 2023 (doi:10.1038/s43247-023-00857-8), Supplementary Tables 1, 3, 4, 8: H2.
- Bauer et al. 2007 via Collins et al. 2013, Section 5.3: nitrate (mass basis taken as kg N).
- Short-lived components (short-term O3, sulfate, nitrate) use tau_O3 = 0.267 yr of Fuglestvedt et
  al. 2010 (status `fallback` where the source treats them as instantaneous); at annual
  resolution this only moves forcing within the first year.

### With a prospective scenario

`ar6_core` evaluates every `CO2Release` component (emitted CO2, uptake, oxidation CO2) and the
CO2 of the climate–carbon feedback either with the AR6 impulse response (analytic) or with a
`TabulatedCO2Response` (annual RF of 1 kg CO2, interpolated linearly, numerical convolution on
the 0.1-yr grid; AGTP with the AR6 temperature response integrated exactly over each step).
`dynamic_characterization/ar6_prospective.py` builds that response from the Watanabe et al.
(2026) scenario data (CO2 radiative efficiency of the emission year x RCP impulse response) and
gives CH4 and N2O the scenario radiative efficiencies, with the AR6 adjustments
(`tropospheric_adjustment` in `ar6_parameters.json`), for `prospective_method="ar6"`. With the AR6
CO2 response sampled annually, the tabulated path reproduces the analytic results within 0.05 %.
The default `prospective_method="watanabe2026"` takes CO2, CH4 and N2O from
`prospective/watanabe2026.py` (port of the paper's code) and uses the paper's CO2 response for
all other gases.
