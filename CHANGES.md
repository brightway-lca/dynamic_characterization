## [Unreleased]

Version 2.0.0. The call syntax of 1.x is unchanged; the results change. See "Migrating to 2.0" in the usage documentation.

### Changed (breaking: results)
* The default characterization functions reproduce the IPCC AR6 emission metrics (WG1 Ch. 7, Tables 7.15 and 7.SM.7): climate-carbon feedback for non-CO2 gases (Gasser et al. 2017), fossil and non-fossil CH4 with the CO2 from oxidation, N2O with its ozone and CH4 effects, the CFC-11/12 adjustment, the SF6 lifetime of Table 7.15, AR6 radiative efficiencies of CO2, CH4 and N2O, and annual forcing that adds up to AGWP(H), with the forcing of the first year stamped at the emission date. GWP100 then matches the static IPCC 2021 factors, e.g. fossil CH4 29.8 (1.x: 26.7), N2O 273 (263.6), CF4 7380 (7058); 184 of the 189 flows of the ecoinvent 3.12 IPCC 2021 GWP100 method agree within 1 %.
* CO, VOC and H2 include their indirect effects, from published parameter sets (`ipcc_ar6/data/precursor_parameters.json`): CO (Fry et al. 2012 multi-model mean, GWP100 1.63, plus 1.57 oxidation CO2 if fossil), VOC (Fry et al. 2012, 4.50 per kg VOC), H2 (Warwick et al. 2023, 11.4; or Sand et al. 2023); NO/NO2/"Nitrogen oxides" (Fry et al. 2012) with optional nitrate aerosol on request.
* The public names `ipcc_ar6.characterize_co2`, `characterize_co2_uptake`, `characterize_ch4`, `characterize_n2o` and `characterize_co` are the AR6 functions (`characterize_ch4`: fossil CH4; `characterize_co`: fossil CO with its indirect effects).
* Prospective metrics (`pGWP`, `pGTP`, `prospective_radiative_forcing`): CO2, CH4 and N2O follow Barbosa Watanabe & Cherubini (2026), including the indirect effects and the climate-carbon feedback (1.x: direct forcing only), with two errors of the paper's published code corrected (feedback boxes 2 and 3 decaying from box 1; CO2 impulse response chosen by scenario position instead of RCP). All other gases keep their AR6/literature forcing, with their CO2 terms following the scenario. `prospective.agwp`, `prospective.agtp` and `prospective.radiative_forcing` keep their signatures and use this method. `time_varying_re=True` lets the radiative efficiencies follow the scenario over the response; horizons beyond 100 years are truncated with a warning; missing radiative efficiencies (REMIND CH4 after 2100) take the last available value.

### Added
* `characterization_options` of `characterize` and `create_characterization_functions_from_method` (class `CharacterizationOptions`): `climate_carbon_feedback`, `ccf_parameters` (Gasser et al. 2017 mean, upper, lower), `biogenic_oxidation_co2`, `ch4_oxidation_yield`, `unspecified_origin`, `flow_overrides`, `include_precursors`, `precursor_parameter_sets`, `include_nox`, `nox_nitrate`, `precursor_ccf`, and `prospective_method` (`"watanabe2026"` or `"ar6"`, the AR6 recipe with scenario inputs).
* `compare_with_static(method, time_horizon, characterization_options)`: table of static factor vs dynamic GWP per flow.
* New modules `ipcc_ar6.ar6_core` (pulse responses; analytic AGWP/AGTP, AR6 numerical climate-carbon feedback, tabulated CO2 responses), `ipcc_ar6.gases`, `prospective.watanabe2026`, `options`, `ar6_dispatch`, `ar6_prospective`, `flows`; data file `ipcc_ar6/data/ar6_parameters.json` (both data files generated by `dev/build_ar6_data.py`).

### Fixed
* CAS numbers are normalised before flows are matched to the halocarbon data. Zero padding (`000075-38-7` vs `75-38-7`) and stray whitespace no longer prevent a match; in ecoinvent 3.12 this adds "Vinylidene fluoride" and "Methyl vinyl ketone", which were left uncharacterized. HFO-1234ze(E), listed in the data with a CAS typo (`29188-24-9`), is also found under its correct CAS `29118-24-9`.
* `pGTP` of gases outside the scenario data used the integrated radiative forcing as if it were an AGTP and applied the emitted amount twice (e.g. CF4 pGTP100 ≈ 150 000 per kg).
* The documentation cited the wrong reference for the prospective module (a 2015 paper instead of Barbosa Watanabe & Cherubini 2026).

### Removed
* The 1.x characterization functions and `ipcc_ar6/data/decay_multipliers.json`, with the notebooks that generated it.

## [1.4.3] - (2026-08-21)
* Fixed prospective RF being constrained to after 2030 even if earlier data exists

## [1.4.2] - (2026-08-14)
* The error raised when a prospective metric is calculated without a scenario now explains what to do: full import path, a copy-pasteable `set_scenario` call, and the available IAM-SSP-RCP combinations.
* Fixed `TypeError: ... got an unexpected keyword argument 'time_varying_re'` for the `pGWP` and `pGTP` metrics: `time_varying_re` was passed to every characterization function, including the IPCC AR6 fallback functions (CO and the GHGs from `decay_multipliers.json`), which don't accept it.

## [1.4.1] - (2026-08-03)
* Fixed a regression introduced in 1.4.0 where ordinary CO2 emission flows (e.g. `Carbon dioxide, fossil` emitted to any air subcategory) got no default characterization function and were silently skipped, understating dynamic climate scores by roughly an order of magnitude. `characterize_uptake=False` now only suppresses the uptake functions instead of all CO2 characterization.
